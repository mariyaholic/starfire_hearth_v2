# Fleet travel

Idle civilian fleets travel along open deep-space lanes. Select Fleet travel on the galaxy or system screen to choose a destination and inspect its journey time. Travel advances when you end a turn; the report announces each arrival. The journey time includes every lane in the route, and a fleet already in transit shows its remaining time.

Stars beyond the current charts appear as numbered unknown markers. A ship reveals a system on arrival; its planets still need to be surveyed. Routes may use known systems as intermediate stops and may end at one unknown star. Busy ships cannot depart, and ships in transit cannot survey, colonise or build outposts. The slowest ship sets a fleet’s speed. Each leg takes its distance divided by half that speed, rounded up to a whole turn. Deep-space travel adds no Noise. A fleet cannot replace its route while in transit. Beacon lanes remain closed in this preview.
