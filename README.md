# Starfire Hearth

Hello! This is Starfire Hearth, my turn-based space 4X for PC and mobile. It is built in Godot 4 with GDScript in a clean modern-flat style. You lead a slowboat colony a hundred years into the Long Silence, the century since the Sol relay stopped transmitting. This one is close to my heart, and I hope you enjoy your time in it as much as I enjoy making it.

**Status:** milestone M1 (First Light): Scenario 1 plays from the title to the debrief on PC and in a phone browser. An M2A Exploration Preview is there too: launch it from Campaign and it adds civilian interstellar travel, frontier surveys, an outpost and a new colony. It does not complete Scenario 2 or unlock later campaign chapters.

## Play

- You need Godot **4.7.2-stable**; the exact version is pinned in `.godot-version`.
- Run `godot --path .` from the repository root to start the game.
- From the title screen, select **Scenario 1** and play through to the debrief.
- The M2A Exploration Preview lives in Campaign. Opt a survey probe into automatic exploration, stop at the next system, or take a detour to The Shroud. After preview victory, **Continue expedition** keeps your original completion date.
